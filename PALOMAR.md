## What Palomar would and would not establish

Would: that the Solution proves exactly the recorded Challenge statements with the three standard axioms, replayed in Lean's kernel and in NanoDa; that an automated review found no blocking mismatch between the statements and the informal account. Would not: novelty (declared unknown in `formalization.yaml`), the prose parts of the repository (the cocycle-row derivation, the finite-fibre reduction, Vampire's target equivalences, the classification of base-dependent extensions), or the general-ring form of Theorem F, none of which is in the Challenge.
