Topic: 1518: one-generated (1518+3862)-magmas are the Z/3 shift; constant-coefficient cohomology cannot refute 47/614/817/3862

Following up on the November 2024 discussion in *Austin pairs* (Terry's [485133886](https://leanprover.zulipchat.com/#narrow/channel/458659-Equational/topic/Austin.20pairs/near/485133886), [485146097](https://leanprover.zulipchat.com/#narrow/channel/458659-Equational/topic/Austin.20pairs/near/485146097), [485148288](https://leanprover.zulipchat.com/#narrow/channel/458659-Equational/topic/Austin.20pairs/near/485148288); Pace's [485154828](https://leanprover.zulipchat.com/#narrow/channel/458659-Equational/topic/Austin.20pairs/near/485154828); Matthew's [485027669](https://leanprover.zulipchat.com/#narrow/channel/458659-Equational/topic/Austin.20pairs/near/485027669)) on why magma cohomology refuted 879 ⇒ 4065 but nothing from 1518. Two statements; I would like to know whether either is known. Everything is at https://github.com/carlok/magma-1518-obstruction-lean (Lean files, Vampire proofs, scripts, certificates, a PDF note); the README gives every statement with its trust level and the tools that confirm it.

**Theorem A.** In any magma satisfying 1518 and 3862, with $$S x = x\diamond x$$: for all $$u,v\in\{x, Sx, S^2x\}$$, $$u\diamond v = S v$$, and $$S^3 x = x$$. So one-generated (1518+3862)-magmas are trivial or the $$\mathbb Z/3$$ shift. This is 485148288 with one target instead of four and no finiteness. Core Lean, no axioms for the table and the closure of words; the nine identities were first found by Vampire from the two laws in milliseconds. Without 3862 it fails: `Refutation939` is itself one-generated.

**Theorem B.** For the $$\mathbb Z/3$$ shift and every finite $$M$$ with $$\alpha,\beta$$ making $$\alpha s+\beta t$$ a 1518-magma, every 1518-cocycle is a coboundary: all constant-coefficient 1518-extensions of the shift are direct products. Route: finiteness makes $$\beta$$ invertible, so every fibre is a module over $$\mathbb Z[b]/(b^5+b^3-b^2-1)$$, and there are $$P,Q$$ over that ring with $$EP+QD=I_9$$ for the coboundary matrix $$E$$ and cocycle matrix $$D$$. That identity is kernel-checked (`propext` only); the rows and the reduction are prose.

**Consequence.** Since the four targets are equivalent in finite 1518-magmas (Vampire), a constant-coefficient extension of a finite 1518-magma violates one of 47, 614, 817, 3862 only if the base already does. The boundary is exactly constant coefficients: `Refutation939` is an extension of the shift by $$\mathbb Z/5$$ with $$\alpha_{x,y},\beta_{x,y}$$ varying with the base pair. $$H^2$$ is nonzero over other bases, and such extensions do refute two-variable laws (1518 ⇒ 632 and ⇒ 879 at carrier 6), never the one-variable targets.

**Checks, briefly.** A: Vampire, core Lean, brute force on all 1518-magmas to size 6. Census sizes 2–8 (now corroboration only): Mace4, z3, Lean `bv_decide` with kernel-`decide` controls. B: Python and Lean kernel on the identities, two independent derivations of the rows, 39 prime specialisations. Not in Lean: the target equivalences and the assembly of the consequence.

**Questions.** (1) Is Theorem A known? It is a short equational fact and may be folklore. (2) Is the vanishing of $$H^2_{1518}$$ over the shift for all finite fibres written down anywhere? (3) Has anyone computed the cohomology with base-dependent coefficients over the shift, so that the six 15-element models are the generic case of something?
