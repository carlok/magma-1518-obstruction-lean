# magma-1518-obstruction-lean

Lean 4-checked results about the Equational Theories Project (ETP) law 1518, `x = (y ◇ y) ◇ (x ◇ (y ◇ x))`:

1. **One-generated (1518 + 3862)-magmas are trivial or the Z/3 shift.** In any magma satisfying 1518 and 3862 (`x ◇ x = (x ◇ (x ◇ x)) ◇ x`), write `S x = x ◇ x`. For every `x` and all `u, v ∈ {x, S x, S (S x)}`, `u ◇ v = S v`, and `S (S (S x)) = x`. So the submagma generated by `x` is that set, with the shift's multiplication table, and it has one element or three. No finiteness is assumed. This is the conjecture stated by Terence Tao on the Lean Zulip on 2024-11-29 ([message 485148288](https://leanprover.zulipchat.com/#narrow/channel/458659-Equational/topic/Austin.20pairs/near/485148288)), with one target law instead of four and without finiteness. **Core Lean, no axioms** for the table and the closure (`lean/OneGenerated1518.lean`).
2. **Constant-coefficient magma cohomology cannot refute `1518 ⇒ 47, 614, 817, 3862` from any finite base.** Over the Z/3 shift every 1518-cocycle with values in a finite 1518-fibre is a coboundary (`H² = 0`), so every constant-coefficient 1518-extension of the shift is a direct product; combined with 1, an extension of a finite 1518-magma violates one of the four targets only if the base already does. The algebraic core (two certificate identities over `Z[b]/(b⁵ + b³ − b² − 1)`) is kernel-checked (`lean/L2Cert.lean`, `lean/H2Cert.lean`, axiom `propext` only); the reduction from finite fibres to that ring and the derivation of the cocycle equations are written proofs, not Lean.

What this is not: not a new implication or non-implication (all of them are settled by the ETP), not a statement about extensions whose coefficients vary with the base pair (the ETP's 15-element countermodel `Refutation939` is exactly such an extension of the shift by Z/5), and not a claim of novelty: the searchable venues were checked and nothing was found, which is different from a proof that nothing exists. Full statements, proofs, verification table, prior art and limits: `paper/l2_note.pdf`. A first-year-level tutorial of every prerequisite: `paper/prerequisites.pdf`.

## Statements

Notation. Laws are numbered as in the ETP (`teorth/equational_theories` @ `88088faa`). `S x = x ◇ x`. An *extension* of a magma `G` by an abelian group `M` with `α, β ∈ End(M)` and `f : G × G → M` is the magma on `G × M` with `(x,s) ◇ (y,t) = (x ◇ y, αs + βt + f(x,y))` (blueprint chapter "Magma cohomology"); the coefficients are constant. A *1518-fibre* is `(M, α, β)` such that `s ◇ t = αs + βt` satisfies 1518. `f` is an *E-cocycle* when the extension satisfies `E`, given that `G` and the fibre do; a *coboundary* is `f(x,y) = g(x ◇ y) − αg(x) − βg(y)`.

**Theorem A (table).** In every magma satisfying 1518 and 3862, for every `x` and all `u, v ∈ T = {x, S x, S S x}`: `u ◇ v = S v`, and `S S S x = x`. Hence `T` is closed under `◇` and carries the multiplication table of the cyclic shift. *Lean: `OneGenerated1518.table`, `B_B`; no axioms.*

**Corollary A′ (classification).** In every magma satisfying 1518 and 3862, every word in `x` lies in `T`, so the submagma generated by `x` is `T`; and either `S x = x = S S x` or `x, S x, S S x` are pairwise distinct. Every one-generated magma satisfying 1518 and 3862 is the trivial magma or the Z/3 shift `x ◇ y = y + 1`. *Lean: `words_in_T` (no axioms), `one_or_three` (standard axioms).* Tao's conjecture of 2024-11-29 asked this for finite magmas satisfying 1518 and all of 47, 614, 817, 3862; here 3862 alone and no finiteness. The target cannot be dropped: the ETP's `Refutation939` is a one-generated 1518-magma with 15 elements violating 3862.

**Proposition A″ (targets).** Under 1518 together with left injectivity and left surjectivity, which hold in every finite 1518-magma, the laws 47, 614, 817, 3862 are pairwise equivalent. Without finiteness, 3862 implies each of the other three, and 47 and 614 are equivalent. *Vampire; not Lean (`atp/imp_*.p`).*

**Lemma B0 (finite fibres).** If `M` is a finite abelian group and `(M, α, β)` is a 1518-fibre, then `β` is invertible, `α = β⁻¹ − β²`, and `(β² + 1)(β³ − 1) = 0`; so `M` is a module over `R = Z[b]/(b⁵ + b³ − b² − 1)` with `b ↦ β` and `a := b⁴ − b ↦ α`. Conversely every `R`-module is a 1518-fibre. Only injectivity of `β` is used. *Written proof (`paper/l2_note.pdf`, Step 1).*

**Theorem B (H² vanishes over the shift).** Let `G` be the Z/3 shift and `(M, α, β)` a finite 1518-fibre. Every 1518-cocycle `f : G × G → M` is a coboundary. Consequently every constant-coefficient 1518-extension of `G` by such a fibre is isomorphic, via `(x,s) ↦ (x, s + g(x))`, to the direct product `G × (M, αs + βt)`, and satisfies every law that both factors satisfy. *Algebraic core in Lean: `H2.homotopy` (`E·P + Q·D = I₉` over `R`, axiom `propext` only); rows and reduction: written proof.*

**Theorem B′ (containment).** With `G` the Z/3 shift or the one-element magma and `(M, α, β)` a finite 1518-fibre, every 1518-cocycle is a 47-, 614-, 817- and 3862-cocycle. *Algebraic core in Lean: `L2.cert_47`, `cert_614`, `cert_817`, `cert_3862` (`C·D₁₅₁₈ = D_E'` over `R`, `propext` only), plus `cert_359` as a control.*

**Corollary C (census form).** If a constant-coefficient extension of a finite 1518-magma `H` by a finite 1518-fibre refutes `1518 ⇒ E'` for `E' ∈ {47, 614, 817, 3862}`, then `H` contains a one-generated submagma with at least 9 elements. *Uses the census (sizes 2 to 8) and Tao's reduction of one-variable targets to one-generated bases; superseded by D.*

**Theorem D (full obstruction).** Let `H` be a finite 1518-magma, `(M, α, β)` a finite 1518-fibre, and `f` a 1518-cocycle on `H`. If `H ×_f M` violates one of 47, 614, 817, 3862, then `H` already violates it. Hence constant-coefficient abelian cohomology never refutes any of these four implications from any finite base. *Assembly of A′, A″, B, B′: written proof (`paper/l2_note.pdf`, Theorem 6.2; `paper/L1_NOTE.md`).*

**Proposition E (the boundary).** (i) The 13 finite non-implications `1518 ⇏ E'` that no constant-coefficient extension of the shift can witness are exactly `E' ∈ {47, 65, 375, 614, 632, 679, 817, 872, 879, 1491, 3862, 3915, 4118}`; the other 4676 are refuted by the shift itself (4347) or by a finite linear 1518-magma alone (329). (ii) `H²_1518(G, M)` is nonzero for other bases, already for the right projection on two elements and for every 1518-base of sizes 4 and 5; such extensions refute `1518 ⇒ 632` and `1518 ⇒ 879` at carrier 6 (base `[[0,1,2],[0,1,2],[1,0,2]]`, fibre Z/2). (iii) `Refutation939` is an extension of the shift by Z/5 with base-dependent coefficients `α_{x,y} ∈ {1,2,4}`, `β_{x,y} ∈ {1,2,3,4}`; by Theorem B no relabelling makes them constant. *Computation (`scripts/`), with the ETP finite graph fetched 2026-09-04.*

**Census (corroboration).** There is no one-generated 1518-magma of size 2, 4, 5, 6, 7 or 8, and the one-generated ones of size 3 are the two labelled shifts. *Lean `bv_decide` (`lean/census/`), Mace4, z3.* Made unnecessary by A′.

## How result 1 was found

Vampire 5.1.0 proved the nine product identities from the two laws in milliseconds (`atp/eq_*.proof`, 9 to 32 lines, superposition and demodulation only). The Lean file is a hand transcription of those proofs, three to seven rewrite steps each. Hypothesis minimisation (`atp/min_*.p`): 3862 alone suffices; 47, 614 or 817 alone suffice only with left injectivity, which holds in finite 1518-magmas; without any target the closure is not provable and `Refutation939` is a one-generated counterexample.
