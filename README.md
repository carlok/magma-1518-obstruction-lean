# magma-1518-obstruction-lean

Lean 4-checked results about the Equational Theories Project (ETP) law 1518, `x = (y ◇ y) ◇ (x ◇ (y ◇ x))`:

1. **One-generated (1518 + 3862)-magmas are trivial or the Z/3 shift.** In any magma satisfying 1518 and 3862 (`x ◇ x = (x ◇ (x ◇ x)) ◇ x`), write `S x = x ◇ x`. For every `x` and all `u, v ∈ {x, S x, S (S x)}`, `u ◇ v = S v`, and `S (S (S x)) = x`. So the submagma generated by `x` is that set, with the shift's multiplication table, and it has one element or three. No finiteness is assumed. This is the conjecture stated by Terence Tao on the Lean Zulip on 2024-11-29 ([message 485148288](https://leanprover.zulipchat.com/#narrow/channel/458659-Equational/topic/Austin.20pairs/near/485148288)), proved here from one target law instead of four. **Core Lean, no axioms** for the table and the closure (`lean/OneGenerated1518.lean`).
2. **Constant-coefficient magma cohomology cannot refute `1518 ⇒ 47, 614, 817, 3862` from any finite base.** Over the Z/3 shift every 1518-cocycle with values in a finite 1518-fibre is a coboundary (`H² = 0`), so every constant-coefficient 1518-extension of the shift is a direct product; combined with 1, an extension of a finite 1518-magma violates one of the four targets only if the base already does. The algebraic core (two certificate identities over `Z[b]/(b⁵ + b³ − b² − 1)`) is kernel-checked (`lean/L2Cert.lean`, `lean/H2Cert.lean`, axiom `propext` only); the reduction from finite fibres to that ring and the derivation of the cocycle equations are written proofs, not Lean.
3. **An explicit family of finite 1518-magmas violating 47, 614, 817, 3862, and the classification of base-dependent extensions of the shift.** For any commutative ring `R` with `1/2` and an element `i` with `i² = −1`, and any nonzero `R`-module `M`, an explicit table on `Z/3 × M` satisfies 1518 and violates the four targets; the ETP's 15-element countermodels are its two smallest members, and it continues with 27, 39, 51, 75, … elements. Up to fibre rescaling, the base-dependent `Z/p`-extensions of the shift satisfying 1518 form exactly 8 classes for `p ≡ 1 mod 4` and 4 for `p ≡ 3 mod 4`, and only the family and its conjugate refute. **Exact arithmetic in `Q(i)`; Lean kernel `decide`, no axioms, for the 15- and 39-element members (`lean/FamilyF5.lean`, `lean/FamilyF13.lean`); Gröbner bases over `Q` and over every `F_p`, `p ≤ 257`.**

What this is not: not a new implication or non-implication (all of them are settled by the ETP; result 3 gives new finite models of known non-implications), and not a claim of novelty: the searchable venues were checked and nothing was found, which is different from a proof that nothing exists. Full statements, proofs, verification table, prior art and limits: `paper/l2_note.pdf`. A first-year-level tutorial of every prerequisite: `paper/prerequisites.pdf`.

## Statements

Notation. Laws are numbered as in the ETP (`teorth/equational_theories` @ `88088faa`). `S x = x ◇ x`. An *extension* of a magma `G` by an abelian group `M` with `α, β ∈ End(M)` and `f : G × G → M` is the magma on `G × M` with `(x,s) ◇ (y,t) = (x ◇ y, αs + βt + f(x,y))` (blueprint chapter "Magma cohomology"); the coefficients are constant. A *1518-fibre* is `(M, α, β)` such that `s ◇ t = αs + βt` satisfies 1518. `f` is an *E-cocycle* when the extension satisfies `E`, given that `G` and the fibre do; a *coboundary* is `f(x,y) = g(x ◇ y) − αg(x) − βg(y)`.

**Theorem A (table).** In every magma satisfying 1518 and 3862, for every `x` and all `u, v ∈ T = {x, S x, S S x}`: `u ◇ v = S v`, and `S S S x = x`. Hence `T` is closed under `◇` and carries the multiplication table of the cyclic shift. *Lean: `OneGenerated1518.table`, `B_B`; no axioms.*

**Corollary A′ (classification).** In every magma satisfying 1518 and 3862, every word in `x` lies in `T`, so the submagma generated by `x` is `T`; and either `S x = x = S S x` or `x, S x, S S x` are pairwise distinct. Every one-generated magma satisfying 1518 and 3862 is the trivial magma or the Z/3 shift `x ◇ y = y + 1`. *Lean: `words_in_T` (no axioms), `one_or_three` (standard axioms).* Tao's conjecture of 2024-11-29 assumed 1518 together with all of 47, 614, 817 and 3862, and stated that the only nontrivial model generated by a single generator is the Z/3 shift up to isomorphism; it carries no finiteness hypothesis. The improvement here is 3862 alone, one target instead of four. The target cannot be dropped: the ETP's `Refutation939` is a one-generated 1518-magma with 15 elements violating 3862.

**Proposition A″ (targets).** Under 1518 together with left injectivity and left surjectivity, which hold in every finite 1518-magma, the laws 47, 614, 817, 3862 are pairwise equivalent. This re-derives an observation of Bruno Le Floch (Lean Zulip, Equational stream, October 2025): in a finite 1518-magma the squaring map and all left multiplications are bijective, and the single-variable targets are then equivalent to each other and to `S S S x = x`. Without finiteness, 3862 implies each of the other three, and 47 and 614 are equivalent; that is Matthew Bolan's observation from Prover9 on the same thread, 2024-11-29 ([message 485153888](https://leanprover.zulipchat.com/#narrow/channel/458659-Equational/topic/Austin.20pairs/near/485153888)), 47 and 614 via 1518 → 359. *Vampire; not Lean (`atp/imp_*.p`).*

**Lemma B0 (finite fibres).** If `M` is a finite abelian group and `(M, α, β)` is a 1518-fibre, then `β` is invertible, `α = β⁻¹ − β²`, and `(β² + 1)(β³ − 1) = 0`; so `M` is a module over `R = Z[b]/(b⁵ + b³ − b² − 1)` with `b ↦ β` and `a := b⁴ − b ↦ α`. Conversely every `R`-module is a 1518-fibre. Only injectivity of `β` is used. *Written proof (`paper/l2_note.pdf`, Step 1).*

**Theorem B (H² vanishes over the shift).** Let `G` be the Z/3 shift and `(M, α, β)` a finite 1518-fibre. Every 1518-cocycle `f : G × G → M` is a coboundary. Consequently every constant-coefficient 1518-extension of `G` by such a fibre is isomorphic, via `(x,s) ↦ (x, s + g(x))`, to the direct product `G × (M, αs + βt)`, and satisfies every law that both factors satisfy. *Algebraic core in Lean: `H2.homotopy` (`E·P + Q·D = I₉` over `R`, axiom `propext` only); rows and reduction: written proof.*

**Theorem B′ (containment).** With `G` the Z/3 shift or the one-element magma and `(M, α, β)` a finite 1518-fibre, every 1518-cocycle is a 47-, 614-, 817- and 3862-cocycle. *Algebraic core in Lean: `L2.cert_47`, `cert_614`, `cert_817`, `cert_3862` (`C·D₁₅₁₈ = D_E'` over `R`, `propext` only), plus `cert_359` as a control.*

**Corollary C (census form).** If a constant-coefficient extension of a finite 1518-magma `H` by a finite 1518-fibre refutes `1518 ⇒ E'` for `E' ∈ {47, 614, 817, 3862}`, then `H` contains a one-generated submagma with at least 9 elements. *Uses the census (sizes 2 to 8) and Tao's reduction of one-variable targets to one-generated bases; superseded by D.*

**Theorem D (full obstruction).** Let `H` be a finite 1518-magma, `(M, α, β)` a finite 1518-fibre, and `f` a 1518-cocycle on `H`. If `H ×_f M` violates one of 47, 614, 817, 3862, then `H` already violates it. Hence constant-coefficient abelian cohomology never refutes any of these four implications from any finite base. *Assembly of A′, A″, B, B′: written proof (`paper/l2_note.pdf`, Theorem 6.2; `paper/L1_NOTE.md`).*

**Proposition E (the boundary).** (i) The 13 finite non-implications `1518 ⇏ E'` that no constant-coefficient extension of the shift can witness are exactly `E' ∈ {47, 65, 375, 614, 632, 679, 817, 872, 879, 1491, 3862, 3915, 4118}`; the other 4676 are refuted by the shift itself (4347) or by a finite linear 1518-magma alone (329). (ii) `H²_1518(G, M)` is nonzero for other bases, already for the right projection on two elements and for every 1518-base of sizes 4 and 5; such extensions refute `1518 ⇒ 632` and `1518 ⇒ 879` at carrier 6 (base `[[0,1,2],[0,1,2],[1,0,2]]`, fibre Z/2). (iii) `Refutation939` is an extension of the shift by Z/5 with base-dependent coefficients `α_{x,y} ∈ {1,2,4}`, `β_{x,y} ∈ {1,2,3,4}`; by Theorem B no relabelling makes them constant. *Computation (`scripts/`), with the ETP finite graph fetched 2026-09-04.*

**Theorem F (explicit family).** Let `R` be a commutative ring in which 2 is invertible and `i ∈ R` satisfies `i² = −1`, and let `M ≠ 0` be an `R`-module. On `Z/3 × M` let `(x,s) ◇ (y,t) = (y+1, a[x][y]·s + b[x][y]·t)` with

```
a = [ -(1+i)/2  -1/2      (1-i)/2 ]     b = [ 1         (1+i)/2  -(1+i)  ]
    [ (1-i)/2   -(1+i)/2  1+i     ]         [ 1/2       1        -2i     ]
    [ 1+i       (1-i)/2   2i      ]         [ (1+i)/2   1/2      -2(1+i) ]
```

Then 1518 holds and 47, 614, 817, 3862 all fail. Squaring is an endomorphism with `S³(x,s) = (x, i·s)`, of order exactly 12. Sizes `3|M|`: 15 (`M = F₅`: Le Floch's models 5 and 6), 27 (`F₉`), 39, 51, 75 (`Z/25`), 87, 111, 123, 147 (`F₄₉`), … *Exact arithmetic in `Q(i)` (`scripts/basedep_family.py`): the two 1518 identity families hold, and each target fails by a coefficient that is a unit of `Z[i, 1/2]`. Lean kernel `decide`, no axioms, for `M = F₅` and `F₁₃` (`lean/FamilyF5.lean`, `lean/FamilyF13.lean`: 1518, the four failures, `S³ ≠ id`, `S¹² = id`). Brute force for `F₉` and `Z/25`.*

**Theorem F′ (classification over the shift).** Consider extensions `(x,s) ◇ (y,t) = (y+1, a[x][y]·s + b[x][y]·t + c[x][y])` of the shift by `F_p`, `p ≥ 5`, satisfying 1518. Then `a` is determined by `b`, `c` ranges over a homogeneous linear space, and whether the targets hold depends only on `(a, b)`. Fibre rescaling `s ↦ λ_x·s` acts freely on the pairs `(a, b)`; the orbits are exactly 8 for `p ≡ 1 mod 4` (the direct product, three rational ones permuted by the base rotation, a Galois-conjugate pair with `D = 1`, and the conjugate pair of Theorem F with `D = ±i`, where `D` is the scalar by which `S³` acts on the fibre) and the 4 rational ones for `p ≡ 3 mod 4`. Only the Theorem F pair violates the targets, so the construction gives countermodels exactly when `p ≡ 1 mod 4`. *Exhaustive enumeration for `p ≤ 61` (`scripts/basedep_direct.py`; the counts `8(p−1)²` and `4(p−1)²` were recorded before `p = 37` was run and held through 61); Gröbner bases of degree 8 over `Q` and over every `F_p`, `5 ≤ p ≤ 257` (`scripts/basedep_groebner2.py`, `basedep_groebner_modp.py`, sympy 1.14); the lift to `Q(i)` by CRT over five primes, verified exactly.*

**Census (corroboration).** There is no one-generated 1518-magma of size 2, 4, 5, 6, 7 or 8, and the one-generated ones of size 3 are the two labelled shifts. *Lean `bv_decide` (`lean/census/`), Mace4, z3.* Made unnecessary by A′.

## Palomar package

The repository root is a Lake project without dependencies (`lakefile.toml`, `lake-manifest.json`) carrying the Challenge/Solution pair of the [Palomar registry](https://palomar-registry.org/). `Challenge.lean` states, in core Lean with no imports, Theorem A (`Magma1518.table`, `cube`, `words_in_T`, `one_or_three`) and the F₅ and F₁₃ members of Theorem F defined by their coefficient matrices on `Z/3 × F_p` (`familyF5_law1518`, `familyF5_refutes`, `familyF5_squaring_order` and the same for F₁₃); its module docstring is the informal account. `Solution.lean` repeats the definitions and proves the ten declarations from `lean/OneGenerated1518.lean` and by kernel `decide`; `comparator.json` lists them with the three standard axioms permitted; `formalization.yaml` records provenance, sources, classification, automation, limitations and review status. Both Lean files are generated by `scripts/gen_palomar.py` from the ETP law text and the family's exact tables, so the laws are not hand-transcribed. Comparator, run locally with the pinned `lean4export` and Lean's kernel, accepts the pair (record in `PALOMAR.md`). Readiness only: nothing has been submitted or registered.

## How result 1 was found

Vampire 5.1.0 proved the nine product identities from the two laws in milliseconds (`atp/eq_*.proof`, 9 to 32 lines, superposition and demodulation only). The Lean file is a hand transcription of those proofs, three to seven rewrite steps each. Hypothesis minimisation (`atp/min_*.p`): 3862 alone suffices; 47, 614 or 817 alone suffice only with left injectivity, which holds in finite 1518-magmas; without any target the closure is not provable and `Refutation939` is a one-generated counterexample.
